# Espelho demo script

## 1. Paste — 20 seconds

Click **Use example**, then **Create twin**.

Say:

> “This is not a generic buyer persona. Everything Jordan knows is bounded by evidence from the supplied history.”

Point to **Known**, **Likely**, and **Unknown**, especially the unknown budget.

## 2. Deliberately fail — 30 seconds

Click **Start rehearsal** and say:

> “Jordan, our platform has dashboards, automatic scores, advanced analytics, and several CRM integrations.”

Let Jordan challenge the feature-heavy pitch. End the rehearsal.

## 3. Show the evidence — 25 seconds

On the analysis screen, say:

> “The coaching does not invent a confidence score. It connects my exact behavior to Jordan's own words and a research principle.”

Point to the customer quote and the adaptive-selling citation.

## 4. Rewind and improve — 30 seconds

Click **Retry from this moment** and say:

> “Fair. Forget the dashboard. What is the hardest objection your sellers fail to handle under pressure?”

After Jordan answers, say:

> “Let us use that objection now. I will show you the exact moment a seller loses control, then let them retry it immediately.”

## Closing line

> “We are not simulating a buyer persona. We are rehearsing tomorrow's conversation.”
